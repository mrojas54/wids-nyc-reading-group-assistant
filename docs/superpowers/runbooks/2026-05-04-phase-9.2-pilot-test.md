## Edge cases

- **SMTP rate limit (2 magic-links/hour/project):** if step 2 hits "email rate limit exceeded," wait an hour or use the cookie-injection workaround documented in the operator's Supabase memory. This is a debugging aid, not part of the test.
- **Forgotten revert (step 7):** worst case is meeting #6 sits at `status='scheduled'` until the next `/wids-schedule-reading-group` run, which overwrites it anyway. Step ordering puts revert (7) before log append (8), so writing the log forces you past the revert.
- **Unexpected meeting status:** if `flip-rsvp` aborts with `unexpected status=...`, meeting #6 is in some state other than `prep` or `scheduled` (e.g., `done`). Investigate before proceeding — don't override; the cycle has likely advanced past pilot-test stage.
