[[BEGIN:_doc]]
Plain-text twin of welcome-availability.html — the multipart/alternative body.

Reproduced from the handoff README's verbatim block, with its bracket
placeholders swapped for this repo's Mustache tokens.

WRAPPING. Prose paragraphs are stored as one long logical line each and are
wrapped to 68 columns by scripts/welcome_availability._wrap_txt after
substitution. Do not hand-wrap them back — the README's pre-wrapped copy was
laid out around a short "[Member]" placeholder, and line-by-line wrapping
cannot re-flow a paragraph already broken for a different token width.
Structural lines (section headers, the aligned Cadence/Evenings/Your hosts
block, the bullets, the bare URLs) stay one per line; a line with no spaces
is never wrapped, which is how the URLs survive intact.

BLOCK MARKERS. [[BEGIN:<name>]] / [[END:<name>]] are consumed by the
composer, which drops a section here whenever the same block is toggled off
in the HTML, so the two bodies cannot drift. They never survive composition —
a marker in a sent email means the body was substituted, not composed.

GREETING. Both bodies read "Hey Queen," (handoff Open Question 5), so neither
carries a first-name token. Keep them identical if either changes. The twin
has no header at all; it opens at the greeting.

One deviation from the handoff's verbatim block: the quote attribution sits
on its own line rather than trailing the quote, so a long quote can never
push the wrap into the author's name. The HTML marks that attribution
white-space:nowrap for the same reason.
